## App Description

### Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
MAM - Meditation & Wellness is your complete spiritual companion for daily sadhana, meditation, yoga, and inner growth. Rooted in timeless Indian spiritual traditions and designed for modern practitioners, MAM helps you build a consistent practice that transforms your mind, body, and spirit.

GUIDED MEDITATIONS
Choose from a rich library of 500+ guided meditation sessions led by experienced teachers. Whether you are a beginner learning to sit still or an advanced practitioner deepening your awareness, MAM offers sessions ranging from 5 minutes to 60 minutes across styles including Vipassana, Transcendental, Body Scan, Loving-Kindness, and Breath Awareness.

STRUCTURED COURSES
Follow step-by-step courses designed to take you from curiosity to mastery. Each course includes video and audio lessons with progress tracking, so you always know where you left off. Courses cover meditation fundamentals, pranayama breathing techniques, yoga philosophy, stress management, better sleep, and spiritual growth.

PRANAYAMA & BREATHING EXERCISES
Master the ancient science of breath control with dedicated pranayama sessions. Practice Anulom Vilom, Kapalbhati, Bhramari, and Ujjayi breathing with guided audio, visual timers, and round counters. Understand the purpose and benefits of each technique with detailed instruction.

MEDITATION TIMER
Prefer unguided practice? Use the customizable meditation timer with ambient sounds — temple bells, flowing river, forest rain, ocean waves, and Om chanting. Set your duration, choose your soundscape, and sit in stillness. Every completed session is logged automatically.

DAILY SADHANA TRACKING
Build unbreakable habits with the My Journey dashboard. Track multiple daily practices — Meditation, Exercise, Cold Shower, Early Wakeup — with visual streak counters, weekly heatmaps, and monthly performance insights. Watch your streak grow and stay motivated with milestone celebrations.

SPIRITUAL QUOTES & INSPIRATION
Start each day with a carefully curated spiritual quote from teachers and traditions across the world. Quotes refresh daily and can be bookmarked for reflection. Find wisdom from the Bhagavad Gita, Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, and contemporary spiritual masters.

LIVE EVENTS & SATSANGS
Join live meditation sessions, satsangs, yoga workshops, and spiritual discourses. Register for upcoming events, receive reminders, and access replays of past sessions. Connect with a community of practitioners walking the same path.

CONTENT DIRECTORY
Explore a searchable library of bhajans, guided meditations, satsang recordings, and spiritual talks. Bookmark your favorites, track your listening history, and discover new content curated for your practice level.

STREAK TRACKING & GAMIFICATION
Stay consistent with visual streak tracking, daily check-ins, and progress milestones. Your journey dashboard shows your longest streak, total meditation minutes, sessions completed, and current level — turning discipline into a rewarding daily ritual.

PREMIUM FEATURES
Unlock the full MAM experience with Premium. Get access to the complete meditation library, all courses, advanced pranayama sessions, offline downloads, live event replays, detailed insights, and an ad-free experience. Choose Monthly (₹199) or Annual (₹1,499 — save 37%) plans.

Your spiritual journey begins with a single breath. Download MAM today and commit to a practice that brings lasting peace, clarity, and well-being into your life.
```
